Fixli connects people who need something repaired at home (**customers**) with independent repair professionals (**professionals**). This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and what you can do.

## 1. What we collect

**Everyone**
- **Account:** your name, mobile number, country, language, and a password (stored only as a one-way hash; we cannot read it).
- **Your device:** the phone model and system name shown in "Your devices", a push-notification token (so we can notify you), and your IP address (for rate limits and security logs).
- **Crash reports:** when the app or server fails, a report is sent to our error-tracking service (Sentry). We remove bodies, tokens, phone numbers, emails and addresses before it leaves our systems.
- **Usage statistics, only if you turn them on** (Language & appearance > "Help improve Fixli"): anonymous events such as which screens are used, tied to a random id made on your phone, never to your account. They never contain your name, number, address, location or messages. This switch is **off** until you turn it on.

**Customers**
- **Addresses** you save (typed text; the phone's position only if you tap "use my current location").
- **Requests:** what you describe (text, voice notes and their written transcript, photos), the trade and problem you chose, when you want it done, prices you receive and accept, photos taken before and after work, extra-work requests, ratings and reviews, favourites, people you block, and any report or dispute you send with its evidence.
- **Trusted contacts** you add for emergencies (name and number). Only add people who agree to it. Today the app records them; texts to them are sent only once that feature is switched on in the app.

**Professionals**
- **Profile:** photo, short description, years of experience, languages, trades, the area you work from (a point you set and a travel distance), working hours, usual prices, portfolio photos.
- **Identity check:** your national ID number, photos of the front and back of the ID, and a selfie. These are looked at **by people on our verification team**; there is no automatic ID or face recognition. They are kept in private storage and shown only through short-lived links to staff who need them, and each viewing is recorded.
- **Work:** requests sent to you, prices you offer, jobs, job photos, whether you are online, the cash you report receiving, the fee you owe Fixli, and payments you report to us.
- **Live location:** while you are **on your way** to a booked job, your phone sends its position so the customer can see you coming. We keep those points for [30] days, then delete them. We do not follow you at other times.

**Chat.** Messages and photos exchanged between a customer and a professional about a booked job. The app flags phone numbers typed into chat. Our staff can read a conversation only to handle a dispute or a safety report, and each reading is recorded.

## 2. Why we use it

To match customers with nearby professionals; show prices and let both sides agree; keep the customer's exact address and phone number private until a job is booked; keep a record of what was agreed and done; calculate and collect Fixli's fee from professionals; check professionals' identity; send the notifications you need; resolve disputes; keep people safe (reports, blocks, emergency screen); stop fraud and abuse; fix problems and improve Fixli; and meet legal duties.

**Automated help.** When you add a description, a voice note or photos to a request, they may be sent to an AI service (currently Google Gemini) to suggest the right trade and problem and to turn speech into text. We do not send your name, number or address with them. The suggestions are only suggestions: you decide.

## 3. Who sees it

- **The other person on a job.** A professional first sees the area, the problem and the photos, not your exact address or number. After you book them (or they book you), the address and phone number are revealed for that job only.
- **Fixli staff**, only what their role needs. Staff sign-in needs a second factor, and the things they look at or change are recorded in an audit log.
- **Companies that run Fixli for us:** hosting and database; file storage; push-notification delivery (Apple, Google, Expo); crash reports (Sentry); Google Gemini for the AI help above; an analytics provider **only if you switched it on**; and a text-message provider **once the emergency-contact texts are switched on** ([provider to be named]).
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

## 4. Cash only

Fixli does not take or hold your money. Jobs are paid in cash directly between customer and professional; there is no card payment, no wallet and no escrow today. Fixli records that a cash payment was reported and confirmed, and the fee the professional owes.

## 5. How long we keep it

- While your account is open: your account, requests, jobs and chats.
- Live-location points: [30] days.
- **When you delete your account** (Account > Security > Delete account, or the web page at fixli.pk/delete-account): we sign you out everywhere and cancel your open requests and offers; we delete your saved addresses, trusted contacts, favourites, profile photo, and (for professionals) ID number, ID photos, selfie and portfolio photos; and we remove your name, number and email from your profile. You can see and change your details in the app; delete your account in the app or on the web; turn usage statistics on or off; turn off location, camera, microphone and notification permissions in your phone settings (some features then stop working); and ask us at privacy@fixli.pk for a copy of your data or to correct it. ## 7. Security

Data travels encrypted; identity documents and job photos are in private storage; passwords are hashed; staff access is limited by role, uses a second factor, and is logged; and sign-in attempts are rate-limited. No system is perfectly safe; tell us at privacy@fixli.pk if you think your account is at risk.

Fixli is for people 18 and over.
