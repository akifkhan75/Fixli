# How to delete your Fixli account and data

You can delete your account yourself, in the app, in about a minute.

## In the app

1. Open Fixli and sign in.
2. Open the **Account** tab, scroll to **Security**, and tap **Delete account**.
3. Enter your password and confirm.

You cannot delete the account **during a job** or while you **owe Fixli money** (professionals). Finish or cancel the job, or pay what is owed, then try again.

## What happens

- You are signed out on every device and the account can no longer be used.
- Your open requests and prices are cancelled.
- **Deleted:** your saved addresses, trusted contacts, favourites, profile photo and, for professionals, your ID number, ID photos, selfie and portfolio photos. Your name, mobile number and email are removed from the profile.
- **Kept, with your name replaced by "Deleted user":** finished jobs with their chats and photos, ratings, disputes, payment and fee records, and audit entries, for [number] years because the law and fair dispute handling require it. After that they are deleted or fully anonymised.
- Your mobile number can be used to register again.

See section 5 of the Privacy Policy for the full list.

## You cannot open the app?

If you lost your phone or cannot sign in, write to **privacy@fixli.pk** from the email on your account or tell us your mobile number, and we will verify it is you (we may call you) and delete the account for you. We reply within [30] days.
